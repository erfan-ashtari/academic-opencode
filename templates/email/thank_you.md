# Thank You Note Template

## Subject
Thank You for [Reason]

Dear [Title] [Last Name],

I wanted to express my sincere gratitude for [Specific Reason].

[Personal paragraph about the impact - 2-3 sentences]

Your [specific help: guidance/feedback/mentorship/support] has been invaluable to [specific outcome]. I particularly appreciated [specific detail].

[Optional: Future connection - "I hope to stay in touch" or "I look forward to our continued collaboration"]

Thank you again for your generosity and support.

With best wishes,

[Your Name]

---

## Variants

### After Conference Presentation
```
Dear Prof. [Last Name],

Thank you for your insightful question during my presentation at [Conference]. Your suggestion about [Specific Point] gave me valuable ideas for future work.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss this further.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### After Receiving Feedback
```
Dear Prof. [Last Name],

Thank you for taking the time to review my paper and provide detailed feedback. Your comments on [Specific Section] were particularly helpful in improving the manuscript.

I have incorporated your suggestions and believe the paper is much stronger as a result.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
```

### After Mentorship
```
Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I am writing to thank you for your mentorship during [Period]. Your guidance on [Specific Aspect] has been instrumental in my development as a researcher.

I am grateful for the time you invested in my growth and hope to make you proud.

With appreciation,
[Your Name]
```

---

## Tips

- Send within 1-2 weeks
- Be specific about what helped
- Keep it brief (under 150 words)
- Be genuine, not generic
- Follow up later with updates
