# Research Inquiry Email Template

## Subject
Inquiry About [Paper Title]

## Body

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I hope this email finds you well. I am [Your Name], a [Position] at [Institution], working on [Research Area].

I recently read your paper "[Paper Title]" published in [Journal/Conference] and found your work on [Specific Topic] particularly interesting. Your approach to [Specific Aspect] aligns closely with my current research on [Your Research Topic].

I would like to inquire about [Specific Question]. Specifically:

1. [Question 1]
2. [Question 2]

Any insights you could share would be greatly appreciated. I would also welcome the opportunity to discuss potential collaboration opportunities.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Institution]
[Your Email]
[Your Website (optional)]

---

## Tips

- Keep email under 200 words
- Be specific about what you read
- Ask clear, focused questions
- Include your affiliation and context
- Don't attach files in first email
- Follow up once after 1-2 weeks if no response
