# Collaboration Request Email Template

## Subject
Potential Collaboration on [Research Topic]

Dear Prof. [Last Name],

I am writing to explore the possibility of a research collaboration between our groups.

I am [Your Name], [Position] at [Institution], where I lead research on [Your Research Area]. I have been following your work on [Their Research Area] with great interest, particularly [Specific Paper/Project].

I believe our complementary expertise could lead to meaningful advances in [Research Topic]. Specifically, I envision:

1. [Collaboration Point 1 - what you bring]
2. [Collaboration Point 2 - what they bring]
3. [Joint opportunity]

To give you context, my recent work includes:
- [Your relevant paper 1]
- [Your relevant paper 2]

I would be happy to share more details about our current work and preliminary results. Would you be available for a brief 20-minute call to discuss this further?

Thank you for considering this opportunity.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Institution]
[Your Email]

---

## Tips

- Demonstrate you know their work
- Be clear about mutual benefit
- Keep it concise (under 250 words)
- Suggest next step (call, meeting)
- Include your relevant background
- Don't be pushy or demanding
