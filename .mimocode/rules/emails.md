# Email Writing Rules

## Structure
- **Subject line**: Clear, specific, includes context (course number, project name, paper title)
- **Salutation**: "Dear Prof./Dr. [Last Name]" (use preferred title)
- **Opening**: State purpose in first sentence
- **Body**: Concise, 2-3 paragraphs max
- **Closing**: "Best regards," or "Sincerely," + full name + affiliation/student ID if relevant

## Tone
- Formal but not stiff
- Direct — avoid unnecessary pleasantries
- Grateful when requesting something
- Professional when following up
- Respectful of the recipient's time

## Common Patterns

### Requesting Meeting
```
Subject: Meeting Request — [Topic] — [Course/Project]

Dear Prof. [Name],

I am writing to request a meeting to discuss [specific topic]. I have been working on [brief context] and would appreciate your guidance on [specific question].

I am available [two specific times]. Please let me know if either works, or suggest an alternative.

Best regards,
[Name]
[Student ID / Affiliation]
```

### Following Up
```
Subject: Follow-up: [Original Subject]

Dear Prof. [Name],

I wanted to follow up on my previous email regarding [topic]. I understand you have a busy schedule, but I wanted to ensure this was on your radar.

[Add any new information or context]

Best regards,
[Name]
```

### Thank You (After Meeting)
```
Subject: Thank You — [Meeting Topic]

Dear Prof. [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to meet with me today. I found our discussion about [topic] very helpful.

As discussed, I will [next steps]. I will follow up with [timeline].

Best regards,
[Name]
```

### Paper Submission
```
Subject: Manuscript Submission — [Paper Title] — [Journal/Conference]

Dear Prof./Dr. [Editor Name],

I am writing to submit our manuscript entitled "[Paper Title]" for consideration in [Journal/Conference Name].

[Brief description of the work and its contribution]

All authors have approved the submission, and the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
[Corresponding Author Name]
[Affiliation]
[Email]
```

### Collaboration Request
```
Subject: Research Collaboration — [Topic] — [Your Name]

Dear Prof. [Name],

I am [your name], a [position] at [institution]. I have been following your work on [specific paper/topic] and found it highly relevant to my research on [your topic].

I would like to explore the possibility of a collaboration on [specific idea]. I believe our combined expertise could lead to [potential contribution].

Would you be available for a brief call to discuss this further?

Best regards,
[Name]
[Affiliation]
[Website/ORCID if available]
```

## Best Practices
- Keep emails under 200 words when possible
- Use bullet points for multiple questions
- Attach relevant documents (paper, CV, transcript)
- Proofread before sending
- Include your full name and affiliation in signature
- Follow up once after 5-7 business days if no response
