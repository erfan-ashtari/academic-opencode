# Proposal Writing Rules

## File Handling

**Convert PDFs and Office files to .md before working with them:**

```bash
/convert-document <proposal.pdf>
```

Store in the same directory as the original.

## Grant Proposal Structure

### NSF Style
1. **Project Summary** — 1 page: Overview, Intellectual Merit, Broader Impacts
2. **Project Description** — 15 pages: Introduction, Background, Methods, Timeline, Budget Justification
3. **References** — Not counted in page limit
4. **Biographical Sketch** — PI and Co-PIs
5. **Budget and Budget Justification** — Detailed line items
6. **Data Management Plan** — How data will be shared/preserved

### NIH Style
1. **Project Narrative** — 3 sentences: significance, innovation, investigator
2. **Specific Aims** — 1 page: goals, hypothesis, rationale
3. **Research Strategy** — 12 pages: significance, innovation, approach
4. **Budget Justification** — Detailed line items
5. **Biographical Sketch** — PI and Co-PIs
6. **Facilities and Resources** — Available equipment, space
7. **Letters of Support** — From collaborators, facilities

### Research Proposal (Thesis)
1. **Title and Abstract**
2. **Introduction** — Problem statement, significance
3. **Literature Review** — What's known, what's missing
4. **Research Questions/Hypotheses**
5. **Methodology** — Design, participants, instruments, analysis
6. **Timeline** — Milestones with dates
7. **Expected Contributions**
8. **References**

## Writing Tips
- Lead with impact, not methodology
- Be specific about deliverables
- Include realistic timelines
- Address potential limitations proactively
- Use concrete examples, not abstractions
- Tailor to funder priorities (check their strategic plan)

## Budget Principles
- Justify every line item
- Include inflation for multi-year projects
- Personnel: salary + fringe + summer salary if applicable
- Equipment: items >$5,000
- Travel: conferences + fieldwork
- Other: materials, supplies, participant costs
- Indirect costs: check F&A rate with grants office

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Specific aims are clear and measurable
- [ ] Significance is compelling
- [ ] Innovation is clearly stated
- [ ] Approach is feasible and well-justified
- [ ] Timeline is realistic
- [ ] Budget is justified and reasonable
- [ ] References are current and relevant
- [ ] Follows funder formatting guidelines
- [ ] Page limits respected
- [ ] All required sections included
